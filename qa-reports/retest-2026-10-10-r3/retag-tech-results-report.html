My apartment runs itself, and this one is everything that happens while I am asleep.

Shades close at 9:22. Kitchen shelf lights shut off at 9:50, which is my signal that the day is over. The Pura kicks on lavender. My Eight Sleep adjusts the bed temperature all night based on my body. The humidifier holds the room at 60%. And 45 minutes after sunrise the blackout layer opens first, then the solar shades, so I wake up to the skyline instead of an alarm.

I set all of it up once and it has run every night since. That is the whole point of the hometel.

Save this for when you are ready to automate your own space and send it to whoever you share a bed with.

#smarthome #homeautomation #hometel #eightsleep #sleeproutine
